# Legal and Accounting Review Checklist

This checklist is a handoff aid for the client's lawyer and accountant. It is not legal or accounting advice, and production release should wait for professional approval of the final wording and operational process.

## Scope To Review

- Seller identity: final registered name, address, tax or producer identifiers, contact details, and any required public registry data.
- Primary producer receipt/invoice process: paper receipt for private customers, invoice for companies, e-invoice default, and printed invoice request wording.
- Checkout disclosures: product availability, prices, delivery or pickup options, payment methods, final payable amount, and document acceptance.
- Withdrawal and cancellation wording: exact applicability, exceptions for the product categories, deadlines, refund rules, return conditions, and customer-facing labels.
- Public withdrawal/request function: whether `/rendelessel-kapcsolatos-keres` satisfies the required availability, confirmation, and durable-medium acknowledgement expectations.
- Complaint handling: required response deadline wording, authority details, conciliation board references, and any mandatory platform or contact data.
- Warranty and product complaint wording: required distinction between kellékszavatosság, termékszavatosság, jótállás if applicable, quality complaints, and perishable or hygiene-sensitive products.
- Privacy policy: final controller details, processors, retention periods, payment provider handling, email sending provider, hosting provider, delivery partners, accounting tools, and deletion/anonymization process.
- Email templates: order confirmation, admin notification, contact confirmation, password management, account verification, and withdrawal/request confirmations.

## Technical Evidence In The App

- Checkout stores accepted terms and privacy policy versions on each order.
- Terms/privacy version constants are shown on the public legal pages.
- Logged-in customers can submit an order-related withdrawal or consumer request from their order detail page.
- Guest customers can submit an order-related withdrawal or consumer request using order number and email address.
- Admin order details show submitted withdrawal or consumer requests.
- The system emails both the customer and business contact when a withdrawal or consumer request is recorded.

## Review Outcome To Record

- Reviewer name and role.
- Review date.
- Approved document versions.
- Required wording changes.
- Required business-process changes outside the application.
- Confirmation that release-time production settings, provider names, and contact details are final.
